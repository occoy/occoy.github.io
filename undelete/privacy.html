Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 September 2026

This policy explains how the Jira app Undelete - Recycle Bin ("the app") handles data. The app is provided by Mattia Angelo Brunetti, an individual developer based in Italy, trading as Occoy ("we"). Contact: undeleted.support@gmail.com.

1. Roles

The organisation that installs the app on its Atlassian site (the "customer") is the data controller for its Jira data. We act as a data processor only to provide the app's functionality. As required by Atlassian, we (not Atlassian) are responsible for the app's processing of End User Data.

2. Data the app processes

To make deleted issues restorable, the app stores a copy ("snapshot") of each Jira issue in the projects it protects: issue fields (summary, description, status, labels, custom field values, etc.); comments and worklogs, including their authors' Atlassian account IDs (display names are not stored; they are looked up live when shown); issue links; and names and sizes of attachments (not the files themselves). For deleted issues it also stores the account ID of the user who deleted the issue and the time. The app stores its own settings. It uses no cookies, analytics, tracking or advertising.

3. Storage and access

All data is stored in Atlassian Forge hosted storage within Atlassian's cloud infrastructure, associated with the customer's site. The app qualifies as "Runs on Atlassian": it does not send any data to servers outside Atlassian, and we operate no external servers or databases. Within the customer's site only Jira administrators can view or restore deleted issues. We do not access customer data unless the customer asks us to for support. We do not sell, rent or share data with third parties.

4. Closed Atlassian accounts (right to erasure)

The app follows Atlassian's user privacy guidelines: it reports the Atlassian account IDs present in its stored data to Atlassian's personal data reporting API at least every 7 days. When Atlassian reports that an account has been closed, the app erases that account from all stored data: the account ID is replaced with a placeholder and @mentions of that person become "@former user".

5. Retention and deletion

Snapshots of live issues are updated as issues change and removed when the issue is deleted (the data then moves to the recycle bin). Deleted-issue records are kept for the retention period chosen by the customer (default 365 days, configurable from 7 to 3650 days) and then erased automatically; administrators can also erase items at any time. When the app is uninstalled, Atlassian deletes the app's hosted storage for that site according to Forge's data-retention rules.

6. Legal basis (GDPR)

We process personal data on the customer's behalf and under its instructions (Art. 28 GDPR) to provide the functionality the customer installed. Data subjects should address requests to the customer (the controller); we will assist the customer as needed.

7. Security

Data stays within Atlassian's infrastructure and benefits from Atlassian's platform security, including encryption in transit and at rest. App functions are restricted to Jira administrators. If we become aware of a security incident affecting customer data, we will notify affected customers and Atlassian without undue delay and within 48 hours.

8. Changes

We may update this policy; the current version is always published at this address.
