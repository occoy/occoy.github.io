Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

This policy explains how the Jira app Undelete - Recycle Bin ("the app") handles data. The app is provided by Mattia Angelo Brunetti, sole proprietor of the Italian business MABARIS DI M.A.B. (VAT IT04083540791, REA CZ-233871, Italy), trading as Occoy ("we"). Contact: support@occoy.com.

1. Roles

The organisation that installs the app on its Atlassian site (the "customer") is the data controller for its Jira data. We act as a data processor only to provide the app's functionality. As required by Atlassian, we (not Atlassian) are responsible for the app's processing of End User Data.

2. Data the app processes

To make deleted work items restorable, the app stores a copy ("snapshot") of each Jira work item in the projects it protects: work item fields (summary, description, status, labels, custom field values, etc.); comments and worklogs, including their authors' Atlassian account IDs (display names are not stored; they are looked up live when shown); links; the names and sizes of attachments; and copies of attachment files up to 50 MB each, unless the customer turns attachment backup off. For deleted work items it also stores the account ID of the user who deleted it and the time. For individually deleted comments, worklogs and attachments it stores a copy of the item (text or file, author account ID, date, visibility), the work item it belonged to, the account ID of the user who deleted it and the time. The app stores its own settings. It uses no cookies, analytics, tracking or advertising.

3. Storage and access

All data, including attachment file copies, is stored in Atlassian Forge hosted storage (key-value storage and the Forge Object Store) within Atlassian's cloud infrastructure, associated with the customer's site. The app qualifies as "Runs on Atlassian": it does not send any data to servers outside Atlassian, and we operate no external servers or databases. Within the customer's site only Jira administrators can view or restore deleted items; if a Jira administrator enables it, project administrators can also view and restore the deleted items of the projects they administer (and only those). We do not access customer data unless the customer asks us to for support. We do not sell, rent or share data with third parties.

4. Closed Atlassian accounts (right to erasure)

The app follows Atlassian's user privacy guidelines: it reports the Atlassian account IDs present in its stored data to Atlassian's personal data reporting API at least every 7 days. When Atlassian reports that an account has been closed, the app erases that account from all stored data: the account ID is replaced with a placeholder and @mentions of that person become "@former user".

5. Retention and deletion

Snapshots of live work items are updated as they change and removed when the work item is deleted (the data then moves to the recycle bin). File copies of live attachments are kept while the attachment exists; after a restore the old copy is erased and the restored file is backed up anew. Records of deleted work items, comments, worklogs and attachments (including file copies) are kept for the retention period chosen by the customer (default 365 days, configurable from 7 to 3650 days) and then erased automatically; administrators can also erase items at any time with "Delete permanently". When the app is uninstalled, Atlassian deletes the app's hosted storage for that site according to Forge's data-retention rules.

6. Legal basis (GDPR)

We process personal data on the customer's behalf and under its instructions (Art. 28 GDPR) to provide the functionality the customer installed. Data subjects should address requests to the customer (the controller); we will assist the customer as needed.

7. Security

Data stays within Atlassian's infrastructure and benefits from Atlassian's platform security, including encryption in transit and at rest. App functions are restricted to Jira administrators and, only if a Jira administrator enables it, to project administrators for their own projects; permanent deletion and settings are reserved to Jira administrators. If we become aware of a security incident affecting customer data, we will notify affected customers and Atlassian without undue delay and within 48 hours.
